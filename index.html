Quick Digital Sanction Loan journey

One flow for new customers, and a shorter pre-approved path for existing ones.

New customers: sanction in under 30 minutes

  1. OnboardWeb, app, WhatsApp or partner link; PAN, GSTIN and mobile OTP only
  2. Consent and dataOne-click consent pulls bureau, GST, bank statements (AA), Udyam, e-KYC
  3. Auto-decisionScorecard and policy rules return approve, refer or decline
  4. Instant offerLimit, rate, EMI and 3 tenure options
  5. Closee-KYC, e-sign, e-NACH and penny-drop bank check
  6. DisburseFunds within 24 hours with WhatsApp and SMS status

Existing customers: pre-approved, same-day top-up

  1. Pre-approved offerBehaviour-based limit via app, SMS and RM, refreshed monthly
  2. One-click consentNo fresh documents; GST and bank data refresh in the background
  3. Instant checksDelinquency, bureau-trigger and fraud screens run automatically
  4. Same-day disbursale-sign on existing mandate; funds credited the same day

Credit and underwriting

Risk-based underwriting, automated where data is strong and reviewed by people where it is not.

Key risks and mitigants

  • Machinery, asset and resale: independent FMV, age and LTV caps, GPS tag, insurance, dealer buy-back
  • Machinery, fraud and dealer collusion: invoice and title checks, site visit, dealer scorecard and holdback
  • Digital, default and over-leverage: bureau and cash-flow limits, FOIR up to 55%, promoter guarantee
  • Digital, identity fraud and model drift: e-KYC, statement-tamper checks, device and geo checks, monthly model monitoring

By documentation and vintage

  • Full financials (3+ years): DSCR on audited numbers, 1.25x minimum; full LTV and ticket
  • ITR, GST and bank (2-3 years): cash-flow underwriting on average balance and GST turnover; 70% of verified inflows counted
  • Thin file or under 2 years: promoter bureau, 60% LTV, ticket cap, site visit, co-applicant; start small and step up on clean repayment

Digital data and decisioning

  • Data: commercial and promoter bureau, GST returns, AA bank statements, ITR, Udyam and MCA, e-invoice and e-way bill, UPI and POS flows, device and geo, own repayment history
  • Engine: ~65% straight-through approve or decline; ~25% auto-referred to credit desk; ~10% manual
  • Overrides: authority matrix, logged reason codes, 5% override cap, 5% audit of auto-approvals, champion-challenger models

Early warnings and actions

  • Signals: bounced EMIs and DPD 1-5, bureau score drop, GST filing lapse or turnover down 25%, falling bank balance, idle machine (GPS), insurance lapse, dealer-level delinquency
  • Actions: reminder at T-3; call and field visit at 15 DPD; freeze top-ups; restructure viable cases; repossess and resell via dealers at 90 DPD; tighten cut-offs on weak segments or dealers

Evaluating and lifting returns

  • Measure: RAROC = (yield - cost of funds - opex - expected loss) / capital, by product, channel, dealer and vintage cohort; hurdle ROE 15%+
  • Levers: dealer-led sourcing and digital documents cut cost; priority-sector and co-lending funds lower cost of funds; insurance cross-sell and top-ups add fee income

Competitive and sustainable

  • Benchmark monthly against banks, NBFCs and fintechs; compete on speed, LTV and service, not rate alone
  • Targeted discounts: dealer subvention and rate step-down for on-time payers; no cuts for high-risk grades
  • Guardrail: a pricing committee enforces a RAROC floor per segment
